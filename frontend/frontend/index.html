How The Operations System Works

A transparent breakdown of how System Admin, Supervisors, Managers, and Staff collaborate seamlessly.

01

System Admin Oversight

Registers staff profiles (Name, Photo, Personal Info, Shift, Section), monitors live attendance (Sign In / Sign Out timestamps), and oversees financial totals broken down by section (Bar, Restaurant, Guest House, Shisha).

02

Supervisors & Managers

Oversee section inventory stock levels, set prices, review daily reports from staff, verify end-of-day revenue, and submit consolidated reports directly to the System Admin.

03

Staff Operations & Shifts

Bartenders, Waiters/Waitresses, and Shisha Agents log in during their assigned shift (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night), record sales line items, update stock, and submit structured daily reports.
